# Competition Rules Framework (Draft) — STDSC

> Placeholder framework. Full rules will replace this once confirmed by the organising committee.

## 1. Eligibility

- Open to secondary school students within defined age bands (*to be confirmed*).
- Teams registered by schools across one or more tracks; individual registration is not the primary path.
- Exact team size per track, substitution rules and eligibility verification process: *to be confirmed*.

## 2. Championship Tracks & Formats

### Track A: Debate & Science/Tech Policy
- Team debates in a recognised school-debate style (*exact style to be confirmed*).
- Preliminary rounds → knockout stages.
- Mixture of prepared and impromptu motions covering civic, ethical, and emerging science/technology policy themes.

### Track B: STEM Quiz & Olympiad
- Team-based analytical qualifiers followed by live stage buzzer rounds.
- Core disciplines: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computing.

### Track C: STEM Innovation & Project Showcase
- Team exhibition of working prototypes or applied research models in robotics, software/coding, renewable energy, or community engineering.
- Strict compliance with venue and prototype safety guidelines (no hazardous chemicals, open flames, or unshielded high voltage).

## 3. Adjudication & Technical Judging

- **Debate**: Trained adjudicators using published criteria (content, style, strategy).
- **STEM Olympiad**: Objective scoring rules and moderator panel.
- **STEM Showcase**: Evaluated by STEM educators and engineers on scientific rigor, community relevance, prototype execution, and oral defense.
- Decisions of adjudicators and technical judges are final subject to any published appeal process.

## 4. Conduct & Safety

- Respectful, evidence-based argumentation and academic honesty are required across all tracks.
- Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, or safety violations may lead to sanctions up to disqualification.
- Participants and accompanying adults must follow the Code of Conduct and Safeguarding Policy.

## 5. Results & Awards

- Results published after the event on the official website (team-level or aggregate as appropriate; no unnecessary personal data).
- Awards and recognition categories across Debate and STEM tracks: *to be confirmed*.

## 6. Amendments

Rules may be updated before the event; the version published on the website at the start of the championship will govern.
