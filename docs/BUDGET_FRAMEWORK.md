# Budget Framework (High-Level)

> No specific naira figures are invented here. Categories and assumptions only.

## Cost Categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Venue & logistics | Hall hire, seating, power, security, cleaning |
| Adjudication & training | Adjudicator fees/expenses, training sessions |
| Materials | Ballots, certificates, trophies, printed packs |
| Technology | Domain, hosting, any paid services, AV |
| Safeguarding & compliance | Policy support, any required checks or training |
| Communications | Design, printing, modest marketing |
| Contingency | Buffer for unexpected costs |
| Young Leaders Network | Follow-on workshops / materials (if in scope) |

## Revenue / Support Categories

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Sponsorship | Primary external support; see Sponsor Package |
| School participation | Any registration fees (*to be decided*; keep accessible) |
| Grants / institutional support | Where available |
| In-kind | Venue, printing, professional time |

## Principles

- Transparency with sponsors and partners on use of funds.
- Prioritise participant experience and safeguarding over non-essential costs.
- Maintain a contingency.
- Review budget after each edition for continuous improvement.

*Detailed line-item budget will be prepared once dates, venue and scale are confirmed.*
