# Surulere Teens Debate & STEM Championship (STDSC)  
## Master Project Proposal (Working Draft)

> **Status:** Living document. Confirmed details are stated as fact; unconfirmed items are clearly marked as placeholders.

---

### 1. Vision

To establish a high-quality, annual debate and STEM championship for secondary school students in Surulere (and invited schools), that develops critical thinking, public speaking, scientific inquiry, applied innovation, and civic awareness, while creating a sustained Young Leaders Network & STEM Labs pipeline.

### 2. Objectives

- Deliver a well-run competitive event across three tracks: Parliamentary & Science/Tech Policy Debate, the STEM Quiz & Olympiad, and the STEM Innovation & Project Showcase.
- Enable secondary schools to field teams and develop both debating and practical STEM culture.
- Provide pathways beyond the event through the Young Leaders Network & STEM Labs.
- Operate with the highest standards of child safeguarding, laboratory/prototype safety, and data privacy.
- Build sustainable partnerships with corporate sponsors, STEM foundations, and institutional partners.

### 3. Target Participants

- Secondary school students (exact age bands and eligibility criteria to be confirmed in the competition rules).
- Schools primarily in Surulere Local Government Area, with potential for wider participation as capacity allows.

### 5. Programme Components

1. **Championship Tracks** — Competitive Debate (including Science & Tech Policy motions), STEM Quiz & Olympiad rounds (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computing), and the STEM Innovation & Project Showcase (robotics, coding, applied science prototypes).
2. **School engagement** — registration process, preparation resources, teacher/coach coordination.
3. **Young Leaders Network & STEM Labs** — post-event development, coding/robotics workshops, mentorship and alumni pathways.
4. **Public website** — information, school guidance, sponsor visibility, policies.
5. **Admin system** — secure management of applications, content and results (authenticated only).

### 6. Safeguarding, Lab Safety & Privacy Principles

- Student personal data is never published on the public website.
- Production registration systems will not go live until authentication, access controls and formal policies are in place.
- Physical STEM prototypes must adhere to strict venue and laboratory safety rules.
- A full Safeguarding Policy and Privacy Notice will be published and reviewed.

### 7. Technology Direction

- Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui-compatible components.
- Deployed on Vercel; repository on GitHub.
- Supabase planned for authenticated data storage and access control.

### 8. Success Indicators (examples)

- Number of participating schools, debate squads, Olympiad teams, and STEM showcase prototypes.
- Quality of debates, STEM projects, and participant feedback.
- Retention into the Young Leaders Network & STEM Labs.
- Sponsor and partner engagement.
- Zero serious safeguarding or safety incidents; full policy compliance.
